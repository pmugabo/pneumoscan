How it works

Four steps from radiograph to decision, with a person in the loop whenever the model is unsure.

1

Register & upload

The clinician registers the child and uploads a frontal chest radiograph.

2

Guard the input

A Mahalanobis distance check rejects images unlike the training data.

3

Calibrated decision

A temperature-scaled EfficientNet-B0 decides only above a frozen confidence gate.

4

Specialist referral

Uncertain cases go to a paediatric specialist's prioritised queue with notes and heatmap.

Four outcomes, never a silent guess

Every radiograph ends in exactly one explicit state. Only two of them are autonomous decisions.
